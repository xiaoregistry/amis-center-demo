    About us

    Helping young people grow through learning

    AMIS Center helps students and young professionals in Phnom Penh gain the languages and skills they need for study, work and life.

    Our name comes from French: Centre d'aide et de motivation des intellectuels pour la société, a centre that supports and motivates learners to contribute to society.
